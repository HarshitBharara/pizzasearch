Behind the slice: how it works

🔤 Frequency ranking

Count how many times each query word appears on a page. Add the counts. The page with the highest total wins.

Score = Σ occurrences of every query term

Example: “pizza” appears 15, 9 and 4 times on three pages, so they rank 1, 2, 3. Easy to fool: repeat a word 500 times and you win.

🕸️ PageRank

A page is important if important pages link to it. Each page splits its rank equally among the pages it links to, and the process repeats until scores settle.

PR(A) = (1−d)/N + d × Σ PR(Ti)/C(Ti)

d = 0.85, N = 6 sites. Dangling pages share their rank with everyone. It ignores the query, so we filter by keyword first, then sort by PageRank.
